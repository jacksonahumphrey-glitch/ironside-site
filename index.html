The Problem

You’re not losing business because the work is bad.

You’re losing it in the gaps — the ones that happen while you’re on a ladder, under a sink, or with another customer.

  • Calls that go unanswered

    Someone calls while you’re mid-job. They hang up. They call the next number.

  • Appointments people forget

    A no-show costs the same hour whether or not anyone shows up to fill it.

  • Leads that go cold

    A new inquiry sits for a day. By the time you reply, they’ve already booked someone else.

  • Customers who’ve gone quiet

    Good past customers who haven’t booked in a year — not lost, just never followed up with.

What We Build

One trunk line. Five taps off it.

Each system runs on its own, and plugs into the way your business already works — no new software for your team to learn.

AI Receptionist

Answers the calls you miss, gets the caller’s name and what they need, and sends anything urgent or clinical straight to you — it doesn’t try to handle those itself.

No‑Show Killer

Automatic reminders sent ahead of every appointment, timed so people actually see them — fewer empty slots, less scrambling to refill your day.

Speed‑to‑Lead

New inquiries get a fast first reply — often within minutes — instead of sitting in an inbox until the customer’s already called someone else.

Review Generator

A short, automatic request sent after a job’s done, while the experience is still fresh — built where it fits your business, since not every practice can take this route.

Database Reactivation

Reaches back out to customers who haven’t booked in a while, so past customers become repeat bookings instead of a list that just sits there.
