The Problem

Most lost business never looks like lost business.

You’re losing it in the gaps, not the work — the ones that happen while you’re on a ladder, under a sink, or with another customer.

  • Missed calls

    Calls that go unanswered

    The phone rings while you’re already on a job. No one answers. They just call the next number instead.

    Caught by the AI Receptionist

  • No-shows

    Appointments that don’t show up

    You blocked the time and prepped for the job — then nobody showed. That hour’s gone either way.

    Caught by the No‑Show Killer

  • Slow replies

    Leads that go cold

    They message wanting help today. If you don’t get back to them until tomorrow, they’ve already found someone who did.

    Caught by Speed‑to‑Lead

  • Missing reviews

    Reviews that never get written

    Great work doesn’t show up online by itself. If nobody asks right after the job, the review never happens.

    Caught by the Review Generator

  • Quiet customers

    Customers who’ve gone quiet

    Some of your best past customers haven’t heard from you in six months. They’re not gone — nobody’s asked them to come back yet.

    Caught by Database Reactivation

  • Busywork

    Repetitive tasks that eat your week

    The same small jobs, over and over. None of them take long on their own, but together they eat hours you could be billing.

    Caught by a Custom Build

Questions

Straight answers.

Anything else, just ask. Send a note and I’ll get back to you.

Is this just an AI receptionist?

No. The receptionist is one of six systems. The others send appointment reminders, reply to new leads fast, ask for reviews after the job, win back past customers, and automate the repetitive tasks that eat your week. Take one, or run them together.

Which system should I start with?

Whichever leak is costing you the most. On our call we look at where work is slipping through (missed calls, no-shows, slow replies, quiet customers) and start there. Most appointment-based businesses land on Answer & Book.

Will the receptionist sound like a robot?

Judge it for yourself. On our Zoom call you can phone the receptionist live and talk to it the way your customers would.

What happens when a call needs me?

Anything urgent, or anything it shouldn’t answer on its own, goes straight through to you. It handles the routine calls so you can handle the ones that need you.

Do I or my staff have to learn new software?

No. Each system is built around the way your business already runs, so there’s nothing new for your team to learn.

Is there a contract?

No. It’s month to month with no term and no lock-in. If it stops paying for itself, you stop paying. Every system can also be bought on its own.

Who do I deal with if something needs changing?

Me, the person who built it. There’s no ticket queue and no account manager in between. Changes and fixes usually happen within days.

What kinds of businesses is this for?

Owner-operated service businesses: trades like HVAC, plumbing and electrical, dental and chiropractic offices, professional practices, restaurants, and home services.
