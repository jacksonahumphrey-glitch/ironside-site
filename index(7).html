The Problem

Most lost business never looks like lost business.

You’re losing it in the gaps, not the work — the ones that happen while you’re on a ladder, under a sink, or with another customer.

  • Calls that go unanswered

    The phone rings while you’re already on a job. No one answers. They just call the next number instead.

  • Appointments that don’t show up

    You blocked the time and prepped for the job — then nobody showed. That hour’s gone either way.

  • Leads that go cold

    They message wanting help today. If you don’t get back to them until tomorrow, they’ve already found someone who did.

  • Customers who’ve gone quiet

    Some of your best past customers haven’t heard from you in six months. They’re not gone — nobody’s asked them to come back yet.

What We Build

Six systems. One job: catch what’s slipping through.

Each one below targets a specific leak in your business — missed calls, no-shows, slow replies, quiet customers. Take just what you need, or run them all together. Either way, there’s nothing new for your team to learn.

AI Receptionist

Never miss another call, even when your hands are full. It answers, finds out who’s calling and what they need, and puts anything urgent straight through to you — so the only “voicemail” your customers ever get is an actual conversation.

No‑Show Killer

Automatic reminders sent ahead of every appointment — so fewer no-shows, a fuller schedule, and none of the paid hours lost to an empty chair.

Speed‑to‑Lead

As soon as someone reaches out, they hear back — often within minutes — so you’re the first business they talk to, not the last one they try.

Review Generator

Great work doesn’t show up online by itself. This automatically asks for a review right after the job — so what people see when they search for you stays fresh. (Not every practice can offer this.)

Database Reactivation

The easiest customer to book again is one who’s already used you before. This automatically reaches back out to the ones who’ve gone quiet, so they rebook instead of quietly going to someone else.
